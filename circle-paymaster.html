Circle Paymaster (ERC‑4337)

Pay gas fees in USDC instead of ETH on EVM chains. Powered by Circle’s Paymaster smart contract using ERC-4337 account abstraction. Enable it in Settings → Gas payment → USDC.

Supported Networks#

Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base

Default gas token is ETH. Enable gasless mode to pay in USDC via Circle Paymaster. A 10% surcharge applies.

✓ Gasless Supported

ARC Network

Gas is always paid in USDC natively — no Paymaster or toggle needed. Sub-second finality included.

⚡ USDC Native

How to Enable#

  1. Open the ⚙ Settings modal from the app header.
  2. Under Gas payment, switch from ETH to USDC.
  3. A teal notice confirms gasless mode is active.
  4. Bridge and Swap will now show ⚡ USDC (gasless) in the route info.

How It Works#

1

UserOperation

Your wallet creates an ERC-4337 UserOperation instead of a regular transaction.

2

USDC Permit

A USDC permit signature authorizes the Paymaster to deduct USDC from your wallet.

3

Bundler

A Bundler submits the UserOperation to the EntryPoint contract on-chain.

4

Settlement

Paymaster pays ETH gas and collects the equivalent + 10% surcharge in USDC from your wallet.
