CCTP Transfer Flow#

Every cross-chain CCTP transfer follows three on-chain stages. Use the source chain's block explorer to track your burn transaction hash at each step.

Step 1 Source chain

Burn

USDC is burned on the source chain. This generates a MessageSent event that Circle's attestation service observes.

Step 2 Circle attestation service

Attestation

Circle's Iris service verifies the burn and signs the message. Status moves from pending to complete once enough block confirmations are reached.

Step 3 Destination chain

Mint

Native USDC is minted on the destination chain. Verify the final balance on the destination block explorer to confirm the transfer is complete.
