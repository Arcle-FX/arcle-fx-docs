Connect Wallet

Arcle FX is non-custodial — you connect your own wallet to interact with the protocol. No account or registration required.

Supported Wallets#

MetaMask

Browser extension or mobile app. Most widely used EVM wallet.

WalletConnect

Scan a QR code to connect any WalletConnect-compatible mobile wallet.

Coinbase Wallet

Coinbase’s self-custody wallet with built-in dApp browser support.

How to Connect#

  1. Click Launch App on the landing page or Connect in the app header.
  2. Select your wallet from the modal (MetaMask, WalletConnect, or Coinbase Wallet).
  3. Approve the connection request in your wallet app.
  4. You’re ready — start bridging, swapping, or sending.

Wallet Management#

Once connected, click the wallet button in the top-right corner to:

  • View your connected address and balance
  • Copy your address to clipboard
  • Disconnect your wallet at any time
