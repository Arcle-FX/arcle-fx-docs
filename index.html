Introduction to Arcle FX

Arcle FX is a non-custodial Cross-Chain FX Protocol built on ARC Network. It enables fast, secure bridging and swapping of stablecoins — USDC, EURC, and cirBTC — across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, and ARC Network using Circle's CCTP infrastructure.

Core Features #

USDC Gas on ARC Network

Pay gas fees in USDC on ARC Network — no ETH required. Predictable, dollar-denominated transaction costs.

Express Bridge (~20s)

CCTP Fast Burn mode delivers cross-chain transfers in approximately 20 seconds. Economy mode offers free transfers with ~20 minute finality.

Non-Custodial & Secure

Arcle FX never holds your funds. All transactions are executed directly through your wallet with on-chain verification via Circle CCTP.

4-Chain Interoperability

Seamlessly move assets across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, and ARC Network. All routes powered by Circle CCTP.

What You Can Do with Arcle FX #

Arcle FX provides three core modules — Bridge, Swap, and Unified Balance — giving you full control over your stablecoins across multiple chains.

  • Bridge: Transfer USDC, EURC, or cirBTC across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, and ARC Network. Choose Express (~20s) or Economy (free, ~20 min) via Circle CCTP v2.
  • Swap: Instantly swap USDC ↔ EURC on the same network, or perform a Cross-Chain Swap with CCTP routing in a single transaction. Liquidity Provider Fee: 0.30%.
  • Unified Balance: View your total USDC balance aggregated across all chains and send it to any address in one click — powered by Circle CCTP. Protocol Fee: 0.10%.
  • Send on ARC Network: Transfer USDC, EURC, or cirBTC directly to any wallet on ARC Network. Finality in under 1 second — gas paid in USDC, no ETH needed.
